Key points (a summary, not a substitute for the Policy)

  • Your rights: you can access, correct, export or delete your personal information: email bot@hxallai.com and we will reply within 15 working days. You can delete your account yourself at any time under Settings → Profile in the apps.

4. Data that is not on our servers: local first#

  • Content on your computer. Agents, conversations, message attachments, files you upload and create, workspaces, agent memories, schedules and workflows, built-in browser data, sign-ins and keys for model services, and local settings are stored on the computer that runs the desktop app. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Web app and mobile apps. They read and show this content from your computer over an end-to-end encrypted connection.   • Joining someone else's server. When you talk to agents on someone else's server, your conversations and the files you send are stored on the computer of that server's owner, and the owner and admins may be able to see them. The owner decides how that data is handled and is responsible for it.

  • Access rules and access activity. The rules you save with Always allow on a confirmation card, and what agents did that is worth knowing (reading and changing files outside their folders, deleting, commands, screenshots, web search, sign-ins, payments and your confirmations; the latest 2,000), are stored on the computer that runs the desktop app (in HXBot's data folder, readable only by your user account) and are not uploaded. You can remove the rules and clear the record under Settings → Access & activity.

11. How we protect personal information#

  • Encryption in transit: the portal, the web app and the APIs all use HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or later). Remote connections are end-to-end encrypted, so the relay cannot read their content.
  • Hashing and minimization: passwords are never stored themselves, only a hash computed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, with a random salt for each account and 100,000 iterations, which cannot be turned back into the password, so we cannot see the password itself; session tokens, invite tokens, machine tokens, codes and one-time sign-in tickets are stored only as hashes; source IP addresses in sign-in records are hashed; network logs do not record query parameters of web addresses.
  • Access control: only authorized operations staff can access the servers. Keys and settings are kept in restricted configuration files and are never written into programs or documents.
  • Abuse prevention: registration, codes and sign-in are rate-limited by email address, mobile number and IP address; codes can be requested at most once a minute, and one mobile number receives at most 10 texted codes a day; a code stops working after 5 wrong entries; each sign-in allows at most 5 password attempts.
  • Backups: account data is backed up regularly. Backup files can be read only by administrators and are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Incident response: we have an incident response plan. If personal information is or may be leaked, altered or lost, we will take remedial measures at once and notify you promptly as required by law (the cause and possible impact, the measures we have taken, what you can do and how to contact us), and report to the competent authorities. Where the GDPR applies, we will notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach unless no notification is required, and notify you without undue delay if the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms. We will also meet the notification duties of US state data breach laws.

No system is completely secure. Please keep your password and your signed-in devices safe, and if you notice anything unusual, change your password and contact us.
