### Added

- A start film: the giraffe with balloons, which becomes the HXBot logo. The desktop app plays it on
  the first start and on the first start after each update; other starts show the logo for 0.6 s.
  The film fits the window (16:9, 4:3 or 9:16) and never cuts the logo or the name. A click or a key
  after the first second skips it. Reduced motion, a missing or damaged film, or a film that does not
  start in 1 s shows the logo. The sound is off; turn it on in Settings · General · Appearance.
- The web client (bot.hxallai.com/app) and the home page (bot.hxallai.com) play the film when this
  browser has not played it in the last 30 days. The film fades into the page. It is muted and does
  not play with reduced motion, a data saver, a 2G connection, blocked storage or a background tab.
- Routines ask for their time zone, and show their next three runs in local time and UTC, with the
  daylight saving changes. Times that a clock change skips run at the first minute after the gap.
- Routines missed while HXBot was closed or the computer slept show how many runs were missed and
  when. They are skipped or run once, as set. A catch-up run that sends, posts, pays or deletes asks
  you first.
- Routine runs show their result: succeeded, no update, not verified, or failed. A result that is
  not verified (for example, a file that it said it saved is not there) shows in the chat and as a
  notification.
- Workflows have app skill, Computer Use and team steps. A turn's tool calls can be saved as a
  workflow, and a routine can run a prompt, a workflow, an app skill or a team task.
- A failed workflow continues from the failed step. External actions that are already done are not
  done again.
- Workflow limits: 10 loops, 100 steps and 600 s per loop by default (Workflows menu · Run limits).
- Teams learn your choices: "Continue" at the time reminder doubles that team's time, and "Wrap up"
  shortens it for the same kind of work. Computer Use learns how long to wait after you stop. You can
  reset both in Settings · Computer Use.
- The team judge can decide alone when its model passed the labeled set and it agreed with you 5
  times in a row. You turn this on from the team card; the 3rd time for the same reason always asks
  you.

### Changed

- Routines that are due in the same minute start one after another over a few seconds, in order.
- A routine that is still running skips its next trigger and shows "Skipped" with the reason.
- After you pause a routine, its editor shows that no trigger is queued.
- Routines from before this version ask once for their time zone. Routines that send, post, pay or
  delete ask before each run.

### Fixed

- A deleted agent no longer comes back after you quit and open HXBot at once, and it no longer stays
  on the web client and on phones. The delete is kept on disk until it is done, and the agent is
  hidden everywhere while you can still undo it.
- A click or a key during the start film no longer reaches the screen behind it.
- Desktop notifications of routines are in your interface language.
