### Fixed

- Many agents no longer freeze the desktop app. The desktop computer gets a turn budget from its
  free memory, power, temperature and main-process delay, and the turns that wait start in a fair
  order: people first, then the critical path of each team, with aging and team shares.
- A team room is no longer paused at its 9th handoff, and no longer runs only 2 pieces of work at
  once.
- The channel list no longer gets slower as the history grows. Each list counted again every
  message of each channel that its reader never read, with JSON reads, on every `channels-changed`
  (89 MB of messages: 209 ms for each list). It now keeps the counts and counts only new messages
  (5 to 12 ms); a message whose kind, author or deleted state changes, a deleted history and a
  rebuilt channel count again.
- The queue check no longer reads every assignment that was ever written for each waiting lane, and
  the channel scheduler reads only the channels with work that is not done.

### Changed

- Teams have no fixed limits on members, time or handoffs. Every handoff is taken and queued, and
  its reply says its place in the queue. A limit counts only when the user names it in the chat.
- A team that makes no progress, loops, spreads (the first time, the lead only gets a reminder),
  works 24 hours or gets 10 spend reminders with no answer starts no new work and asks the user to
  continue, wrap up or stop. The same work that fails twice still stops the team.
